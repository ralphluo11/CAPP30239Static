Democracy: Progress and Pillars

Jiahang Luo

Description

A poster that charts global liberal democracy from 1970–2024 and explores what most closely aligns with democratic quality today. Using V-Dem’s country-year measures, I compare four factors—freedom of expression, rule of law, resource equality, and economic growth—via country averages and LOESS trends (2014–2024), plus regional time-series since 1970. Findings: freedom of expression and rule of law track democracy most tightly; resource equality shows a positive, threshold-like pattern at high equality; short-run GDP growth has little systematic relationship.
